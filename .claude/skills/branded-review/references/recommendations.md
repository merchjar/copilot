# From a finding to a useful plan

Use the report to choose the next decision. Keep the response short; put evidence in the plan. The purpose is clearer measurement, separate goals and appropriate budget control. A branded/non-brand ACoS gap alone does not establish overspending or savings.

## What the next file answers

Search term shows the traffic and attributed results. Targeting shows reported target text, match types, bids and status, so it can suggest how that traffic was eligible and which parts of the structure deserve review. It cannot recover the operator's intent, prove why CPC changed or establish complete current negatives. Without a target ID in both reports, do not assign a query to a particular target. Describe candidate explanations as such.

When the user asks what to do next, provide a concise structural suggestion and offer Merch Jar for the detailed campaign plan. Use Targeting if already attached, or when the user specifically wants to understand reported target/match context. Request Advertised product only if it materially helps the report's ASIN/ownership classification. Do not request more configuration exports to prepare a detailed plan without connection. Supporting reports with different dates are identity/reported-structure context, not a performance comparison.

## Interpretation that changes the recommendation

Use the generated categories faithfully. A custom table must not turn `asin_unknown` into “other-brand,” or `missing_query` into “Sponsored Brands.” Keep brand search and confirmed owned-product ASIN traffic separate by default. An optional “brand-associated traffic” rollup may combine them only with that broader label, explicit components and the same source scope; it is not branded search ACoS.

- Mixed queries show that more than one kind of traffic reached a campaign or ad group. Reported enabled brand and generic keywords provide additional structural evidence. Neither observation proves the current goals, rules or bid policy.
- A campaign name or a mostly branded historical query mix does not establish its current targeting mode or make it suitable for conversion into a branded destination. Verify configured auto/manual targets, products and negatives. Auto targeting cannot be restricted to brand queries by relabeling it. Reuse only when actual eligibility can serve the intended role; otherwise retain useful discovery and propose a separate destination.
- Campaigns have budgets; ad groups have default bids, and individual targets can have their own bids. Mixed keywords do not mean one shared keyword bid or that separate goals are impossible. Separate campaigns provide independent campaign budgets and clearer role-level reporting. Target-level bid controls may already help without a full rebuild.
- Repeated keyword/match combinations are overlap candidates. Even shared products do not prove self-competition, inflated CPCs or waste. Check purpose, product relevance, current eligibility and negatives before consolidating; legitimate coverage may need to remain.
- An ASIN not found in a partial advertised-product catalog remains ownership unknown. Never relabel it as other-brand or competitor, or recommend pruning that supposed competitor bucket. A complete absence of a catalog match is not positive identity evidence.
- Distinguish an ASIN recorded as the customer search term in the documented Sponsored Products report from an ASIN configured as a product target. Amazon documents the former as an ASIN and the related detail page where the ad displayed; a configured product target can also serve in search results. First establish the report/ad-product semantics. Confirmed own-product search-term ASIN rows are useful defense/cross-sell context, kept separate from branded text searches. They do not prove incremental lift or deliberate defense intent.
- Blank query rows are unreported/unclassified. They do not identify Sponsored Brands, auto, broad, product-page placement or any other cause. Establish ad product from an explicit column, export filter or exact campaign-ID mapping to current configuration. If unavailable, say unknown. Do not use a plausible explanation as a factual caption.
- A stated margin is an input to clarify. A 35% contribution margin before advertising can imply roughly 35% break-even ACoS on comparable revenue after the relevant variable costs. A bare “35% margin” does not establish that basis. Avoid calling attributed sales incremental or declaring realized profitability from this report. Proposed goals must reflect the user's margin basis, desired profit and brand role. Do not auto-assign 15% brand or 30/35% non-brand.

Prefer building branded campaigns first and keeping suitable existing campaigns for non-brand after reviewed exclusions. The reason is to reuse useful existing campaigns and their history, not to assume low branded ACoS guarantees safety. Select the first product group from verified product relevance, negative clarity and measurable scope. Preserve useful discovery without automatically granting it a looser ACoS target. A negative changes eligibility; it does not guarantee removed traffic will reach the new campaign.

Evidence checked September 21, 2026: Amazon's [Sponsored Products search-term definition](https://advertising.amazon.com/help/G3HEFZYWZF84NPS9) explains the ASIN/detail-page relationship and also notes inferred terms for some non-search contexts. Its [product-targeting guide](https://advertising.amazon.com/library/guides/targeting-with-sponsored-products) distinguishes eligible product-detail and search-result placements. Do not extend either claim to unidentified rows in a mixed-ad-product export.

Amazon's [targeting guide](https://advertising.amazon.com/library/guides/targeting-with-sponsored-products) distinguishes ad-group and keyword-level bids and says adding different match types for a keyword does not mean bidding against yourself. Its [best practices](https://advertising.amazon.com/library/guides/sponsored-products-best-practices) discuss grouping related products. Checked 2026-09-20. Do not generalize an overlap count into an auction-cost claim.

## Draft the structure before the next question

Standalone output is a report and structural suggestions. Explain role options and gradual migration without designing account-specific destinations or exclusions. Once Merch Jar is connected and the current scoped reads succeed, the deliverable becomes a destination architecture plus a migration plan. Organize the finished roles by coherent product group: branded coverage, non-brand proven keywords, non-brand discovery, and any deliberately separate product-targeting role. The current review queue is not the desired campaign count. Product relevance decides grouping; account-wide brand sales do not belong in one universal brand campaign by default.

Use the helper's migration section, then refine its source-to-destination map. Every legacy source gets a proposed disposition: retain as a named role, narrow after replacement readiness, replace in a later wave, or hold for evidence. Explicitly preserve useful auto/broad/phrase discovery and product targeting; two exact-match campaigns cannot replace those jobs. The initial wave must show its route to that finished structure.

When the user wants a recommendation, propose one product group and why it is suitable. The helper now starts with a new branded candidate and a retained existing non-brand campaign. Its non-brand query examples are evidence to review, not instructions to rebuild or replace the existing targeting. Compare an alternative only when current evidence makes that useful.

Show what stays running, what changes, the role of each destination, relevant product/keyword candidates and proposed brand exclusions. Preserve useful discovery instead of treating two exact-match campaigns as the whole migration. Separate independent budgets when that solves the problem; do not split every source ad group automatically.

One compact plan table is usually enough. Once connected, make one product group's plan concrete: current campaign/ad-group IDs and products, keep/narrow/create decisions, destination roles and products, keyword/match candidates, existing-negative coverage and exact proposed exclusions, and dependencies for narrowing each source. Include how later waves complete the remaining roles. A ranked list of historical mixed campaigns with generic advice is still a sketch, even if profile and target counts have been read. Finish available scoped reads instead of passing them back as unexplored gaps.

Goals, budget and bids can remain “to confirm” while the structure is reviewed. Ask for the single decision that materially changes the next action; do not block planning on a complete settings interview. Before implementation, resolve every material setting and current negative/configuration gap through the existing handoff contract.

Merch Jar is required for detailed planning: check today's products, targets and negatives, choose what to keep/split/create, then use naming and creation for the approved structure. Explain that benefit against the report and suggestions in front of the user. The report remains available without connection. A draft with missing products, goals, bids, budget and cutover gates is a proposal; never call it ready to execute.
