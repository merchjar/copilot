# Current exclusions for connected planning

Detailed campaign, negative and transition planning requires Merch Jar connected and successful current reads for the selected scope. Do not direct customers to download bulk files or provide a bulk-upload alternative.

## Read the selected account

Use the current Connect contract and target/negative listing with `targets:read`, exact profile, complete pagination and explicit campaign/ad-group scope. Check both parent levels and supported keyword/product-negative coverage. A 403 is a permission gap, never an empty negative list. Do not promise unsupported ad products or scopes. Match live negative coverage to the specific destination and legacy scope in the proposed migration.

Retain type, value/expression, match type, ID, campaign/group IDs, own state and parent states. An enabled negative under a paused parent is not evidence of active delivery. Check sibling groups and products before recommending a campaign-wide exclusion. Preserve unknown and unsupported coverage explicitly. If required reads are unavailable or initial sync is incomplete, retain the report and structural suggestions and explain the specific remaining setup step. Do not substitute a manual export for this connected planning step.

## Compare against the intended role

Distinguish already covered, proposed, conflicting and unknown. A phrase exclusion may cover several exact candidates; a bare-brand exact negative is not a phrase-wide brand exclusion. A negative on one sibling group does not cover another. Brand classification approval does not approve the same phrase as a negative, especially for model aliases, cross-brand terms or campaign-level changes affecting products outside the wave.

Reconcile currently enabled Product Ads, ASINs and SKUs with historical advertised-product evidence before choosing destinations. One ASIN can have multiple SKU ads with different states. Review each legacy exclusion for the new destination's intended role instead of copying all negatives blindly. Keep source configuration unchanged until the scoped transition is ready and approved.

Current configuration cannot establish when or why a negative was added. Historical branded traffic and a current branded exclusion can both be valid. Do not declare the exclusion broken from that comparison alone.

## Already supplied files

The default Targeting performance template is not a complete negative inventory. No negative rows does not establish that the account has none. Inspect actual fields and coverage, preserving missing, empty-with-verified-coverage and permission-denied as different states.

An unsolicited bulk workbook can corroborate factual observations or support internal acceptance tests. It does not unlock detailed planning in this skill. Keep any summary brief, then offer the connected path when the user wants a concrete plan. Never produce an upload workbook.

For a supplied English bulk export, campaign/ad-group keyword and product negatives are separate entity types. Preserve source sheet/row, raw expressions and own/parent states. Child names may appear only in informational columns. Unexpected historical match types require verification. The filename token is not a Merch Jar profile ID; verify account identity separately. Blank performance columns are valid configuration-only data, not zero spend. Some exports incorrectly declare their used range as `A1:A1`; inspect actual worksheet rows rather than treating that dimension as proof of an empty file.
