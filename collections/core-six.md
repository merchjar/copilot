Every Merch Jar account starts with these six Segments already installed, disabled. They cover the loop most sellers run by hand: stop paying for search terms that never convert, move bids toward your target ACOS, keep budgets from capping proven campaigns, recover lost impressions on winners, and pause the product ads and targets that have had their chance.

Turn them on in this order: search-term waste first (it only adds negatives), then bid management, then budgets. Give each one a week before the next. Ask your Copilot to "check my segments" to see which are on, or "enable the core six" to walk through them with a preview at every step.
